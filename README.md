# GABA WaterWatch — FNB App of the Year Competition (Android Debug Preview)

GABA WaterWatch is a water-awareness and resident-reporting prototype created by Last Dices (PTY) LTD for the FNB App of the Year 2026 competition.

## Download
See the [Releases tab](../../releases) to download the **Android debug APK**.

## Important safety and functionality notes
- This is an **experimental Android debug-signed build**, not a Play Store release or independently audited medical or municipal service.
- All water incidents and sensor readings are fictional and **no municipality is connected to or endorses the app**.
- Offline user reports are saved on your device. **They are NOT transmitted to a municipality.** Shared live synchronisation requires a later authorised and production-tested backend.
- Water-health guidance links to CDC/WHO references. It is general educational guidance, **not a drinking-water safety determination**.
- Do not submit personal medical information, identifying information, or real infrastructure security data into this competition build.

Source development is maintained separately in the Last Dices GABA repository. For partnerships, refer to lastdicesptyltd.co.za.
