GABA WATERWATCH - 30-SECOND VOICEOVER
FNB APP OF THE YEAR HACKATHON 2026
Company: LAST DICES (PTY) LTD

South Africa loses valuable water while communities struggle to report problems and track responses.

We built GABA WaterWatch, an Android application connecting residents and water operators through one accountable reporting system.

Residents can report burst pipes, water interruptions and other concerns, even when temporarily offline.

Reports synchronise to our cloud backend, receive tracking numbers and support an operator review workflow.

Our demonstration uses synthetic water data, with no live municipal integration.

GABA WaterWatch: Water in sight. Communities in mind.
